The State of Osmanthia

Once a centralized empire under the Long Dynasty, Osmanthia turned to a Republic after a century of partial colonization by the West. That republic proved unstable, as the military strongmen that were relied upon to overthrow the Long Dynasty carved their own realms out of the Long Dynasty's husk.
This era of chaos, called the Warlord Era, is the current era the citizens of Osmanthia find themselves in, with rampant inequality, poverty, corruption and violence.

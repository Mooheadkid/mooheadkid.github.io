The Shia Dynasty and the Jade Statues

Once upon a time, the land now known as Osmanthia found itself in an era of chaos, known only as the Era of Poison, where disease, death and destruction reigned supreme. In this era arose the Jade Emperor and his Five Disciples to combat the corruption of their era. The Jade Emperor organized villages, defended against neighboring kingdoms, and conquered his enemies, while his Disciples fought against the Demonic forces sowing discord amongst the people. In time, the land began to heal, but not before a final confrontation between the overlords of the Demonic Sects, known as the Five Poisons, and the Jade Emperor's new Kingdom, the Battle of the Five Flowers.
During the battle, the most powerful of the Five Poisons, the Spider, struck the Jade Emperor with his sword. Knowing his physical death drew near, the Jade Emperor sealed himself alongside the Spider in a column of jade, trapping the Spider in a perpetual slumber. With this, the Era of Poison drew to a close, and those Five Disciples and their descendants went on to found five sects that influenced the nation throughout its history.
With the defeat of the Spider, and the rout of the other Four Poisons, the Five Disciples of the Jade Emperor entrusted the ruling of the kingdom to the descendants of the Jade Emperor, creating the Shia Dynasty.

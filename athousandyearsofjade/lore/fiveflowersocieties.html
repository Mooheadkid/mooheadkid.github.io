The Five Flower Societies

After the defeat of the Five Poisons at the Battle of the Five Flowers, the Five Disciples went their separate ways. Their descendants would later found the Five Flower Societies at various points in history. These societies are listed below.

  • The Yiguandao

    repesents the roots and leaves of the Way.

  • The Red Spears

    represents the fire and passion of liberty.

  • The Sun Yi An Guild

    represents the bounty of the earth.

  • The Yellow Sand Society

    represents the forging of newness.

  • The Blue Shirt Society

    represents the changing tides of society.

As time passed, and the people forgot the threat of the Five Poisons, the Five Flower Societies began to succumb to the vices of the Five Poisons, becoming enveloped into one Five Flower Society which serves as the powerbase of the remaining Four Poisons.
